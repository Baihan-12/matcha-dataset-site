# MATCHA Website Copy

## Hero

- Dataset Website
- MATCHA: Multi-disorder Mental Health Data in China
- MATCHA is a large-scale Chinese Weibo dataset for mental health research. It links disorder-specific Super-Topic discussions with user timeline posts, supports cross-lingual analysis, and is released under a controlled-access policy.
- 7 DSM-5 disorder groups plus a control cohort
- 47,131 users and 1.8M+ de-identified posts
- 38 symptom labels and 12 life-event labels

## Overview

- MATCHA covers attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder, autism spectrum disorder, bipolar disorder, depressive disorders, generalized anxiety disorder, obsessive-compulsive disorder, and schizophrenia, together with a control group.
- The dataset combines disorder-related Weibo Super-Topic posts (STP) and user homepage posts (HP), enabling behavioral, linguistic, and cross-lingual analyses at scale.
- Public-facing website with summary statistics and figures
- Representative, non-release sample snippets only
- Controlled data access after the anonymity period
- No full raw dataset is hosted on this website

## Access Policy

- The complete dataset is not distributed through this GitHub Pages website.
- Full dataset access requires a signed data use agreement and review by the maintainers.
- During the anonymity period, the full dataset is not available for download or application through this page.
- After the anonymity period ends, qualified academic users may apply for access subject to the agreement and approval process.
- Public materials on this site are limited to overview text, summary statistics, derived figures, and non-sensitive examples.
